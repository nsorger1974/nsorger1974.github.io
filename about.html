Certain Latitude leads joyful trips for women in midlife — to connect with one another and experience cultures far and wide.

The name

Latitude, both ways

Latitude demarcates our place in the world. It is also the room we give ourselves, and in our next act, we have earned it in spades!

As our children have grown up, our daily lives are more flexible, and we have more space to consider what we want for this next chapter in our lives. With the clarity and wisdom of poet Mary Oliver, we can ask ourselves: “What is it you plan to do with your one wild and precious life?”

Certain Latitude is designed for women like us as we dive into this juncture in our lives. We crave adventure. Freedom. Connection. A change of pace. Fresh scenery. New people & stories to share.

So now, it’s our turn. We’ve got the latitude to venture out and explore the world — to widen our perspectives, stretch our minds and expand our world views. What are you waiting for? Join in the fun!

How we travel

What makes us stand out

Our itineraries are personally vetted to ensure we are staying in accommodations that are comfortable but not fancy, always centrally situated and full of local flair, and days built at a pace you can actually enjoy.

  • 10–14 travelers, ages 45–65. A cozy group that fits around one long dining table for shared conversations.
  • We sleep inside the old cities, with a focus on small family-owned hotels that feel like part of the neighborhood rather than fancy international chains.
  • Our time on the road will not exceed four hours on a single day, and will take advantage of high speed rail where available.
  • Daily agendas with flexibility to either get more steps or sit and enjoy an afternoon coffee and pastry.
  • We will dine in vetted restaurants with local specialties and accommodations for all dietary needs.
  • Most travelers will come on their own, but do invite friends to join us too!

Questions we get

Before you ask

Women embarking upon our next act, roughly ages 45–65. Most come alone. A few come with a sister or a friend. Everyone arrives curious and reasonably game, which turns out to be the only thing that matters.

Come solo, or bring a friend — and know that new friends await. The group is capped at fourteen precisely so that no one is ever on the edge of it. Meals are together unless you would rather they weren’t, and there is enough unscheduled time that you can have an afternoon entirely to yourself without anyone taking it personally.

Comfortable walking is the requirement. Medinas are cobbled, occasionally stepped, and best seen on foot — expect two to four miles across a full day, broken up by sitting down and eating. The hikes are gentle to moderate on forest tracks; sneakers are fine and hiking boots are not required. Pace can be adjusted for the group, and there is no shame in taking an afternoon off. If you have a specific concern — knees, altitude, heat — write and ask. We will tell you honestly whether a given trip suits.

Prices are based on two travelers sharing. A room of your own is a $1,000 single supplement. The norm is coming solo and sharing a room; we’ll match you up with a roommate for the trip unless you prefer otherwise.

Riads are small and their rooms vary a great deal, so the exact allocation is set once the group is confirmed — and we will tell you which room is yours before you pack.

Included: all hotels, meals, domestic travel, guides, tips and entrance fees.

Not included: travel insurance, which is required of every traveler, and international flights from your city of origin.

For insurance, compare policies at Squaremouth — an independent marketplace that lets you weigh several insurers side by side. International flights you book yourself, with our recommended routing and timings to work from, so you keep your own miles, seats and flexibility.

Morocco is $3,900 per traveler for the 7-day option and $4,500 for the 9-day, both based on two sharing; a room of your own is $1,000 more. A $750 deposit is due by November 1, 2026, with the balance due January 15, 2027.

Just make a note of any dietary needs when you sign up, and we’ll let the restaurants know in advance. Vegetarian, dairy-free, gluten-free and kosher style are all available options.

For Morocco: cancel in writing on or before December 1, 2026 and everything you have paid is refunded in full. After that date, payments are non-refundable — which is what travel insurance is for, and why we require it. This does not apply if we cancel; see below.

You may pass your place to someone else who meets the group criteria up to 45 days before departure, at no charge. And if we cancel — if the group does not reach ten travelers — we will let you know as early as we can and refund everything, including the deposit.

Join the interest list. When the group opens, list members are offered places first and in order — there are only fourteen, and the first trip is likely to fill from the list alone. Joining commits you to nothing.

A place is held with a deposit. For Morocco that is $750 per traveler, due by November 1, 2026. Full prices, the payment schedule and the cancellation terms are on the Morocco page.
